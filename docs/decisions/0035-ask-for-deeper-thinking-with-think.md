# ADR-0035: Ask for Deeper Thinking With `/think`

- **Date:** 2026-11-16
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Group:** [Architecture](README.md#architecture)

## Decision

Deeper thinking for one turn is asked for with `/think`. Raising effort on its own, from reply-shape signals, is
optional and later.

## Rationale

A command is reliable and costs nothing to detect. A classifier's reliability is unknown, and adaptive thinking already
scales within one effort.
