# ADR-0045: Give the Model Local Time With Zone and Offset

- **Date:** 2026-11-17
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Group:** [Architecture](README.md#architecture)

## Decision

The model gets the local time with its zone and offset, not UTC.

## Rationale

Measured: a reminder for 1:48 am local was confirmed "at 17:48".
