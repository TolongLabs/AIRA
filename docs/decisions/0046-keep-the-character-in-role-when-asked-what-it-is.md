# ADR-0046: Keep the Character in Role When Asked What It Is

- **Date:** 2026-11-17
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Group:** [Architecture](README.md#architecture)

## Decision

A character always plays its persona: asked who or what it is, it answers as the character, such as "a scientist". It
never steps out of role and never claims to be human. This replaces the row that answered "an AI".

## Rationale

The owner wants full roleplay. Answering as the character keeps the role without a claim about being human.
