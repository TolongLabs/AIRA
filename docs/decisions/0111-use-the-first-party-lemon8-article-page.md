# ADR-0111: Use the First-Party Lemon8 Article Page

- **Date:** 2026-11-22
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Group:** [Architecture](README.md#architecture)

## Decision

Use the approved first-party Lemon8 article page and post-owned gallery or video on both hosts.

## Rationale

Both returned an identity-bound article, ranged native WebP and MP4. A reported 48 s video measured 48.467 s.
